Who am I?

Initially, my journey in design began with graphic design.

Over time, I developed the skills to create a wide range of visual designs, including:
- Posters & social media creatives
- T-shirt & apparel designs
- Logos, identity & branding
- Print materials & promotional designs
- Custom graphics & visual concepts

I’m currently expanding my skills in web design and building my personal brand from scratch — turning ideas into designs that stand out.
